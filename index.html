I'm Joe Etherage. My work has three threads. As a clinician, I led behavioral-health departments and programs for the U.S. Air Force, the U.S. Army and NATO's Supreme Headquarters Allied Powers Europe. As a builder, I designed and ran measurement systems: post-deployment screening that assessed 41,000 soldiers, and a suicide-event report that the Department of Defense adopted department-wide. As an overseer, I have directed healthcare inspection work that tests whether systems do what they claim, most recently on generative AI in clinical care.

In 2026 I founded JE Vectors LLC and built Inqura, an evidence-analysis platform for investigations and compliance review, along with ConKurrence, a harness for measuring whether AI judgments hold up. I am interested in AI oversight, healthcare and mental-health AI governance, and evaluation, as an employee or as a consultant.

Systems built

Inqura

2026 · JE Vectors LLC

An evidence-analysis platform for investigations and compliance review, designed and built solo with Claude Code as the engineering agent. Every claim in a finding cites its evidence, every finding carries a confidence level, and the server blocks sign-off until the reviewer has opened the cited evidence.

I wrote the original ten-criteria evidence-evaluation framework, then validated it down to the one-page guide that runs in production: two judgments per piece of evidence (is it relevant; does it have a material limitation), with an explicit materiality threshold. In controlled multi-model tests, rater agreement rose from chance level to substantial. An independent reproduction confirmed the agreement result but not the expert-anchor check, and I am correcting that public claim.

ConKurrence

2026 · JE Vectors LLC

A multi-model evaluation harness (command line, MCP server and web interface; 194+ automated tests) that measures whether AI judgments agree with each other and with expert anchors, so accuracy claims can be tested rather than asserted.
